Privacy Policy

Protecting Your Data & Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Breast Cancer Detection using Deep Learning platform collects, processes, stores, and protects your information while using this application.

Introduction

This application has been developed as a Final Year Engineering Project to demonstrate the use of Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning for breast cancer detection from mammography images. The platform is intended for educational, research, and demonstration purposes only.

We respect every user's privacy and are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of information shared through this website.

By accessing or using this application, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When creating an account, users may provide their name, email address, username, and encrypted password for authentication purposes.

Medical Images

Users may upload mammography images that are processed by the deep learning model solely to generate prediction results.

System Information

Basic technical information such as browser type, operating system, device characteristics, and application logs may be collected to improve performance and reliability.

How Your Information Is Used

The information collected through this platform is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating registered users.
  • Processing uploaded mammogram images using the AI model.
  • Displaying prediction results and confidence scores.
  • Improving system stability, security, and usability.
  • Supporting educational demonstrations and academic research.
  • Preventing unauthorized access and misuse of the platform.

Uploaded Mammography Images

Mammography images uploaded through this platform are processed by the integrated Deep Learning model for the purpose of generating breast cancer prediction results. These images are never intentionally shared with third parties without the user's consent.

Depending on the deployment configuration, uploaded images may either be processed temporarily in memory or stored securely for research, debugging, and performance evaluation. Users should avoid uploading images containing unnecessary personally identifiable information.

The project developers take reasonable precautions to protect uploaded files from unauthorized access; however, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

User Authentication

Registered users authenticate using secure login credentials. Passwords should always be kept confidential and should never be shared with other individuals.

Passwords are stored using secure cryptographic hashing mechanisms rather than plain text wherever supported by the application.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their accounts and for all activities performed using their credentials.

Cookies & Session Management

This application may use browser cookies or secure session tokens to maintain authenticated login sessions and improve the overall user experience.

Login Sessions

Session cookies help identify authenticated users while they navigate through the application.

User Preferences

Certain browser settings may be remembered temporarily to improve usability and accessibility.

Security

Session management mechanisms help protect user accounts against unauthorized access.

Data Retention

User account information is retained only as long as necessary for authentication, educational purposes, or project administration.

Uploaded mammography images may be automatically deleted after processing unless long-term storage has been explicitly enabled by the project administrator.

Temporary application logs may be retained for debugging, performance optimization, and system maintenance.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information or uploaded medical images to advertisers or commercial organizations.

Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law, university regulations, or to protect the security and integrity of the application.

  • No commercial sale of personal information.
  • No sharing for marketing purposes.
  • Disclosure only when legally required.
  • Internal access limited to authorized administrators.

Technologies Used

Flask

Provides secure backend routing, authentication, and application management.

TensorFlow / Keras

Performs AI inference for breast cancer prediction using trained deep learning models.

SQLite Database

Stores user account information and application data required for authentication.

Data Security

Protecting user information is one of our highest priorities. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to safeguard personal information and uploaded medical images against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Access to sensitive information is restricted to authorized administrators responsible for maintaining and improving the application. Authentication mechanisms, encrypted password storage, and secure session management help reduce security risks.

Although we strive to use industry-standard security practices, transmission of information over the Internet can never be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should take appropriate precautions to protect their own devices and login credentials.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and institutional policies, users may have certain rights regarding their personal information.

  • Access information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Request clarification regarding data processing practices.
  • Contact the project administrator regarding privacy concerns.

Third-Party Services

This project may utilize open-source libraries and publicly available software frameworks to provide AI prediction, authentication, user interface components, and application functionality.

Python Ecosystem

Python libraries are used for backend processing, preprocessing, and machine learning operations.

Open Source Libraries

CSS, JavaScript, and AI libraries are integrated to improve functionality, accessibility, and performance.

Deployment Platform

When deployed online, limited technical information such as server logs may be collected by the hosting provider for operational and security purposes.

Children's Privacy

This application is designed primarily for educational, research, and demonstration purposes. It is not intended for direct use by children without appropriate supervision.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If such information is discovered, reasonable efforts will be made to remove it in accordance with applicable policies.

Medical Disclaimer

The predictions generated by this AI system are intended solely as decision-support information for research and educational purposes. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, screening, or treatment.

Users should always consult qualified healthcare professionals for interpretation of mammography images and clinical decision-making. The developers, institution, and contributors are not responsible for medical decisions made solely on the basis of AI-generated predictions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in project functionality, legal requirements, deployment environments, or security improvements.

Updated versions will become effective immediately after publication on this page. Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about how their information is handled.
