Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI model?

The prediction accuracy depends on the trained deep learning model and dataset. Results are intended for research and educational purposes and should always be verified by qualified medical professionals.

Are uploaded images stored?

Uploaded mammogram images are processed only to generate predictions. Permanent storage depends on the deployment configuration chosen by the project administrator.

Can this replace a doctor?

No. The system is an AI-assisted decision support tool and must never be considered a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or clinical judgment.

Clinical decisions must always be made by qualified healthcare professionals after reviewing the patient's complete medical history, imaging studies, laboratory findings, and physical examination.
